Pumpkin Bread

Home

Description

This pumpkin banana bread is a moist, tasty breakfast bread that's easy to make with pumpkin puree, mashed bananas, cinnamon, and pie spice. Perfect with jam, butter, or even cream cheese.

Ingredients

  • 2 ripe bananas, mashed
  • 1 1/3 cups canned pumpkin puree
  • 1/2 cup honey
  • 1/2 cup white sugar
  • 1/3 cup vegetable oil
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 teaspoons pumpkin pie spice
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 3/4 cup raisins (Optional)
  • 1/2 cup walnut pieces (Optional)

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Grease a 9x5-inch loaf pan.
  2. Stir together bananas, pumpkin, honey, sugar, vegetable oil, and eggs in a large bowl. Combine flour, pie spice, baking powder, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt in a separate bowl. Stir flour mixture into banana mixture until just combined. Fold in raisins and walnuts. Pour batter into the prepared pan.
  3. Bake in the preheated oven until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean and the top springs back lightly when touched, 45 minutes to 1 hour. Cool loaf in the pan for 10 minutes before transferring to a wire rack to cool completely.
